## The Challenge

**Current Problems:**
- Students in the ICT department are most likely to attend their classes due to frustrations of not knowing what to wear on campus.
- This problem is caused by financial background and awkwardness of repeating their clothing items when attending leading to lowered self esteem and their marks dropping.
- Social isolation .

**Impact of the Problem:**
- Poor academic perfomance, frustration, and significant inconvenience for sense of belonging in the field / ICT industry .
- Missed classes and academic activities .
- Increased stress and anxiety for students .

---

##  The Solution

**ICT-BRANDED Provides:**
- A robust online ecommerce app.
- Real-time display of t-shirts.
- Digital application to buy and order their t-shirts.


**Benefits:**
- Inclusion
- Significantly improved academic record.
- An enhanced, student-specific experience.

---

## Key Features

The ICT-BRANDED app includes a functional web/mobile application powered by a secure database-driven system and an interactive user interface. 

Core functionalities include:
- **Authentication:** Secure user registration and login.
- **Cart Management:** View real-time available products.
- **History Tracking:** Comprehensive cart history logs.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Powerful tools for administrative personnel to manage orders.
